Method	Kya karta hai	RAG me kahan use
get_client()	Langfuse client deta hai	Application startup
@observe()	Function ko automatically trace/observation karta hai	Full RAG pipeline
start_as_current_observation()	Manually ek child step create karta hai	Retrieval, reranker, guardrail
observation.update()	Existing observation me input/output/metadata add karta hai	Retrieved docs, scores etc.
update_current_trace()	Current trace ki information update karta hai	user/session/tags
update_current_observation()	Current observation ko update karta hai	input/output/metadata
CallbackHandler()	LangChain calls automatically trace karta hai	LLM/chain/retriever calls
flush()	Pending traces Langfuse ko bhejta hai	S
